What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize shopping research around Oopbuy. A useful list records the source link, product description, selected variant, measurements, listed price, and the date each detail was checked. 03 / Oopbuy shopping guide

Four passes.
A clearer product list.

Use this Oopbuy spreadsheet guide as a repeatable research process for shoes, everyday clothing, headwear, and accessories.

01

Choose a purpose

Start with what you need: an everyday sneaker, a warmer layer, a matching set, or a small accessory. Set a total budget before gathering links.

02

Record the variant

Save the exact product URL, size, color, and seller description. Keep similar-looking variants in separate rows so their details do not get mixed up.

03

Check the evidence

Read measurements and material notes. If inspection or QC photos are available, check that they correspond to the selected item and variant.

04

Recheck before paying

Reopen the source listing. Confirm current availability, the payable amount, delivery options, and the return terms relevant to your order.

04 / The comparison desk

Give every row a reason.

An Oopbuy product list becomes more useful when it shows what is known, what is missing, and what needs another check.

Suggested fields for a fashion research spreadsheet
Field What to record Why it helps
Source and date Original listing URL, seller name, date checked Makes it easier to revisit the listing and spot outdated information.
Exact variant Color, size, model reference, and selected options Prevents comparisons between different versions of an item.
Fit and dimensions Garment measurements, shoe sizing notes, or accessory dimensions Supports a fit decision based on more than a size label.
Materials Seller-stated composition and any missing details Separates documented descriptions from visual assumptions.
Photos and evidence Listing photos, available inspection photos, questions to resolve Keeps visual references connected to the item being considered.
Costs and terms Item price, currency, delivery estimates, applicable charges, return terms Helps compare the expected order cost and purchase conditions.

Compare the whole order: item price + domestic delivery + any service charges + international shipping + optional insurance + applicable taxes or duties + any currency-conversion charges. Keep estimated amounts separate from confirmed charges.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize shopping research around Oopbuy. A useful list records the source link, product description, selected variant, measurements, listed price, and the date each detail was checked.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide that links to CAFANS categories. It does not provide an Oopbuy account service, live Oopbuy inventory, or an official Oopbuy spreadsheet download.

Can a spreadsheet verify product authenticity?

No. A spreadsheet, brand name, photograph, or product link does not establish authenticity. Request appropriate provenance and use qualified authentication where needed.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the selected variant’s measurements with a garment or pair of shoes that already fits you. Check measurement units and seller sizing notes rather than relying only on S, M, L, or a familiar shoe-size label.

Does a product’s listed price include the complete order cost?

Do not assume it does. Check item price, domestic delivery, any service charges, international shipping, optional insurance, applicable taxes or duties, and any currency-conversion charges before paying.

Where do the category links lead?

All shopping links on this page lead to CAFANS. Category buttons open the corresponding CAFANS category; general browse buttons open www.cafans.com.
